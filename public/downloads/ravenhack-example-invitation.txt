EXAMPLE TEXT - R@VENHACK INVITATION
Fill in the parts between [ ] and send this email to all players.


Subject: R@venHack - [date] at [time]


R@venHack starts on [date] at [time].

Not an ordinary online meeting, but an escape room in which you and your
team go investigating. Hidden clues, suspicious digital situations, and a
clock that simply keeps ticking.

Exactly what is going to happen, we are keeping to ourselves for now.


You are playing in team [COLOUR].
Remember that colour. You will need it right at the start.


Important: everyone takes part individually.
So: 1 player = 1 laptop = 1 screen. Do not sit down together behind one
computer, not even if you work in the same place.

There is nothing to prepare on the content. On the technical side there
is. Use a laptop or computer and open the game in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Here you can read beforehand what you need:
https://www.meetingmasters.online/en/games-tools/ravenhack/participants

The link to the game is in your calendar invitation.

Make sure you are in on time. Once the starting signal sounds, the clock
is ticking.
