Content

The words the cards are built from, plus the logo that goes in the centre square.

  • Type your words in the text box below, one per line or separated by commas.
  • At least 24 words: a card has 24 squares plus the logo in the middle.
  • More is always allowed, and better. Every card takes its own random 24 from your list, so the longer the list, the more the cards differ.
  • Making changes? Click "Activate this bingo" at the top again afterwards. Links you already shared keep using the old words.
